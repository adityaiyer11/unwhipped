# Unwhipped

Prime Minister's Questions as a deck of fights. Read what was actually said, take a stance, and watch your politics take shape.

A single static page. Open `index.html` or visit the GitHub Pages site.

## Sources and licences

- Transcript text is from Hansard via TheyWorkForYou's ParlParse data, used under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0.
- Members' portraits and posts are from the UK Parliament Members API. Portraits are © UK Parliament, licensed CC BY 3.0.
- Background explainers are written summaries of general knowledge, not Hansard, and are labelled as such on the page.
