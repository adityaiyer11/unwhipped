# Unwhipped

Parliamentary debates as a feed of fights. Read what was actually said, take a stance, and watch your politics take shape.

A single static page. Open `index.html` or visit the GitHub Pages site.

## What is in it

- Prime Minister's Questions, 2 September 2026, card by card.
- A bank of fights from June to September 2026 debates in the UK House of Commons, the House of Lords, the Australian House of Representatives and the House of Commons of Canada.
- Filters by chamber, by world affairs, and by topic. Every card names its debate, date and chamber, and links to the official record.

Every quote on every card is checked word for word against the transcript before it is published. Speeches shown in full may be trimmed around the quoted words on long contributions; the link goes to the complete record.
